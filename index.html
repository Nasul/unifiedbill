How it works
Three steps. Then you are done.
1

Add your billers

Enter your OPPD, MUD, and Cox account numbers once at signup. UnifiedBill remembers them for every payment after that.

2

Link your bank

Securely connect your bank account in seconds through our encrypted bank linking flow. Your credentials are never stored on our servers.

3

Pay your bills

One click pays any bill directly to the biller's bank account. You get an email confirmation the moment the payment is sent.

Payment infrastructure
Money goes directly to billers

No middleman. No holding your money.

When you pay through UnifiedBill, the money transfers directly from your bank account to OPPD, MUD, or Cox via ACH bank transfer — the same method used by Wells Fargo bill pay and other bank bill pay services.

Each payment includes your biller account number in the payment reference field, so billers automatically match the payment to the correct customer account. The money is in the biller's bank account within 1–2 business days.

Payments are processed through Stripe, a licensed payment processor used by Uber, Amazon, and Shopify — holding Money Transmitter Licenses across all US states.
