How it works

Three steps, once a month.

01

Add your billers

Enter your OPPD, MUD, and Cox account numbers once at signup. UnifiedBill remembers them for every payment after that.

02

Link your bank

Securely connect your bank account in seconds through our encrypted bank linking flow. Your credentials are never stored.

03

Pay your bills

One click pays any bill directly to the biller's bank account via ACH transfer. You get an email confirmation instantly.

Payment processing

Payments go directly to billers — no middleman

When you pay through UnifiedBill, the money transfers directly from your bank account to OPPD, MUD, or Cox via ACH bank transfer — the same method used by Wells Fargo bill pay and other bank bill pay services.

Each payment includes your biller account number in the payment reference field, so billers can automatically match the payment to the correct customer account.

Payments are processed through Stripe, a licensed payment processor used by companies like Uber, Amazon, and Shopify.
